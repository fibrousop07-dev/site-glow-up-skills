# AI-art tells and hand-drawn styles

## Contents
1. Visual tells  2. Weak signals  3. Not AI (leave alone)  4. Styles  5. Drawing tips

## 1. Visual tells (score each; 3+ = `high`)
- **Plastic gloss**: everything has the same soft, waxy highlight and rim light.
- **3D blobs / glass shapes / mesh gradients** used as decoration with no subject.
- **Too-smooth skin and surfaces**, no grain, no tool marks, no wear.
- **Impossible details**: extra or fused fingers, mismatched earrings, melted background objects, chairs that merge into tables.
- **Garbled text**: letters that look like letters, signs and labels that say nothing.
- **Perfect symmetry with odd breaks**, repeated patterns that drift.
- **Uniform "cinematic" lighting** (teal and orange, volumetric haze) on mundane subjects.
- **Generic isometric or 3D-cartoon scenes** with the same soft shadow under every object.
- **Dream-like depth of field** applied to flat subjects.
- **Icon sets** with identical gradients and sparkle accents.

## 2. Weak signals (hint only, never sufficient)
PNG text chunks (`parameters`, `prompt`), C2PA / "Content Credentials", sizes of exactly 1024x1024 / 1792x1024, file names like `ChatGPT Image`, `midjourney`, `DALL·E`, `Generated`.

## 3. Not AI (leave alone)
Real photographs of the owner's place, people, product; logos; screenshots; charts with real data; third-party brand artwork you have no rights to change.

**Scanned or photographic textures** (torn posters, peeling paint, concrete, paper grain, collage). Signs of a real scan: irregular physical damage, a consistent grain, flat limited palette, no "subject". A themed site built on this is a deliberate look. Keep it and say so; redrawing it loses what the design is about. Only flag it if it also shows the tells in section 1 (waxy gloss, impossible details, garbled text).

## 4. Styles
| Style | Line | Fill | Texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| ink | dark, slightly uneven, round caps, double stroke on corners | flat, offset 2-4px from the outline | faint paper grain |
| pencil | thin, grey, repeated light strokes, lifted ends | hatch lines, no solid fill | strong grain |
| marker | thick, flat, a little translucent | overlaps darken | none, edge bleed |
| crayon | thick, broken, textured | patchy, leaves gaps | heavy grain |

## 5. Drawing tips
- Draw the **idea** (a wrench, a loaf of bread), not a copy of the AI composition.
- Simplify: 5-15 shapes. Hand drawings omit detail on purpose.
- Let fills miss the outline; let lines overshoot corners.
- Vary stroke width within one drawing, 2px to 5px.
- Keep a 3-color palette from the site; use one accent only.
- Avoid perfect circles and perfectly straight lines. If you need a circle, draw it as a slightly open path.
